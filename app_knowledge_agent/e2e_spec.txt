End-to-End Automation Test Flow (Consumer App → Business App → Kitchen App → Payment)

Test Setup
Launch the Consumer App.
Create an event with:
1 Host
1 Participant
5 Guests
Participant accepts the event invitation.

Phase 1 – Pre-Order (Consumer App)
Host Pre-Order
Add items containing:
Products without options.
Products with options/variants.
Products with modifiers having different VAT rates (3%, 14%, and 17% where applicable).
Include:
Single quantity items.
Multiple quantity items (2x and 3x).
Participant Pre-Order
Repeat the same:
Items without options.
Items with options.
Modifiers with different VAT combinations.
Multiple quantities.
Complete the pre-order.

Phase 2 – Table Assignment (Business App)
Open the created event.
Assign a table.
For all seven people (Host + Participant + 5 Guests):
Add products manually.
Use a combination of:
Products without options
Example:
Coca-Cola
Add quantity 5.
Assign to different guests.
Split the quantity among different guests.
Products with options
Examples:
Pizza with variants.
Products with modifiers.
Modifiers having different VAT rates.
Use:
Quantity 2.
Quantity 3.
Quantity 5.
Perform:
Assign.
Split.
Mixed assignments.
Do not simply add one product per guest.

Phase 3 – Modify Orders (Business App)
Go to the Payment/Order section.
Perform all of the following:
Increase quantity of selected items.
Decrease quantity of selected items.
Remove one product completely.
Split one product using swipe gesture.
Void one already split item.
Example:
Void the entire quantity "2" if 2/3 is the item
Send all items to Kitchen.

Phase 4 – Assign & Split After Sending to Kitchen
Navigate to the Assign/Split page.
Perform:
Assign selected items to different guests.
Split additional items.
Return to the Order page.
Now:
Void another item.
This time choose a split item.
Example:
Void only one item instead of the entire split.

Phase 5 – Kitchen App
Open Kitchen App. Get all items ready
Before serving:
Perform another split operation.
Use a different scenario than previous splits.
If previous split used an item with options, this split should use an item without options (or vice versa).
Serve all items.

Phase 6 – Comp Items
After serving:
Perform multiple Comp operations.
Scenario 1
Comp 2 split item that contains options completely
Scenario 2
Comp one non-split item with options.
Quantity = 2 or 3.
Comp only one quantity.
Scenario 3
Comp another split item.
Example:
Quantity = 3 split between 2
Comp only one quantity.
Scenario 4
Comp one item without options.

Phase 7 – Additional Consumer Orders
Before notifying payment:
Using Consumer App:
Host places additional orders.
Participant places additional orders.
These new orders should contain:
Products with options.
Products without options.
Multiple quantities.
Back in Business App:
Assign some of these newly ordered products to other guests.
Send these new items to Kitchen.
After sending:
Reassign one Host-ordered product to another guest.
Split another Consumer App product.
Go to Kitchen App.
Serve all newly ordered items.
After serving:
Perform one additional split on a Consumer App ordered item.

Phase 8 – Notify Payment
Notify payment for all seven people.

Phase 9 – Consumer App Payment
Host selects Pay for Others.
Pay for:
Two guests.
Apply:
Coupon.
Tip.
Payment Mode:
E-Payment.
Verify:
Coupon applied correctly.
Tip calculated correctly.
Final payable amount is correct.

Phase 10 – Business App Payment
Participant pays for two other guests.
Apply:
Discount.
Tip.
Complete payments using all three payment methods:
Cash.
Card/E-Payment.
Food Voucher (or configured third payment mode).
Validate:
Discount is applied to the overall payable amount for the selected payment, not just an individual item/person.
Tips are calculated correctly.
Payment totals are correct.
Complete all remaining payments.

Phase 11 – Invoice Validation
Verify invoices for:
Host.
Participant.
All guests.
Validate:
Assigned items.
Split items.
Void items.
Comp items.
Discounts.
Coupons.
Tips.
VAT calculations.
Payment methods.
Final totals.
Close the table.

Phase 12 – Review & Invoice (Consumer App)
Host:
Submit a restaurant rating/review.
Open and verify invoice.
Participant:
Skip rating.
Open and verify invoice.
Validate that invoices match the Business App invoices and all calculations remain consistent.

Overall Validation Checklist
Verify throughout the flow:
Assign functionality.
Split functionality.
Void (full and partial).
Comp (full and partial).
Quantity increase/decrease.
Product removal.
Kitchen workflow.
Consumer App ordering after table creation.
Business App reassignment after Kitchen submission.
Payment notification.
Pay for Others.
Coupon application.
Discount calculation.
Tip calculation.
Multiple payment methods.
VAT calculations across mixed VAT items.
Invoice accuracy.
Table closure.
Review submission and skip flow.
